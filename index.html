When Should You Call Bossdawg Tree Service Deseronto?

Unsure whether your tree poses a real threat? Look for these common warning signs on your property.

Noticeable Trunk Lean

If a tree suddenly develops a severe lean or soil around the base is cracking, root failure may be imminent.

Limbs Overhanging Roofline

Heavy branches touching or scraping your shingles can cause structural roof damage and invite pests.

Dead Bark or Fungal Growth

Large patches of peeling bark or mushroom clusters near the trunk indicate internal decay and hollow wood.

Storm Fracture & Cracked V-Crotch

Deep vertical cracks along major trunk forks can cause half the tree to split off during heavy wind.
